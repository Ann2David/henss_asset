About Henss Asset Company

Our Vision is to become a major business vehicle positively impacting economic growth and development in communities across Africa.

It is Our Mission to form and deploy technical collaborations to produce growth and cause development to uplift communities across Africa.

Our Values
We value the commitments we make. We believe in the power of working in collaboration.

Henss Asset Company is a Full - service commercial organisation with a strong asset base and valuable technical partnerships with many years of combined experience.
We are driven to build a legacy of top performance, keeping our commitments, providing value for money, and making positive social impact.

1980 Story
The Henss brand was created By the late Chief Effiom Henry Etim in August, 1980; derived from his middle name, HENRY, and styled with two letter S for distinction.
Henss was initially established as a trading company based in Calabar, Cross River, South-South of Nigeria. Henss grew out of an earlier trading partnership in the 1970s, in which Chief Etim was involved while in his twenties.
Today, Henss has metamorphosed into a holding vehicle for various business interests of the founder’s living family. A true generational legacy.
The brand is reputed for keeping its commitments.
